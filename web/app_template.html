    How to post

    1. Tap Save slides and save each image to your Photos.
    2. In TikTok, tap +, choose Photo, and pick the slides in order.
    3. Tap Copy title and Copy caption, and paste them in.
    4. Add a soft worship or piano sound, then post. Space your posts a few hours apart through the day.

    All Scripture is from the Berean Standard Bible, which is in the public domain, so you can post it freely.
